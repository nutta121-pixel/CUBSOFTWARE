## What is CUB PROTECTOR?

**CUB PROTECTOR** is a **free, full-featured Discord bot** built to handle moderation, protection, engagement, and server management — all from one clean web dashboard.

> No premium tiers. No locked features. No subscriptions. Everything is free, forever.

---

## Smart Auto-Moderation

**Every server faces spam, raids, and abuse. CUB PROTECTOR stops it automatically before it becomes a problem.**

**Spam Detection** — Stops flooding and repeated messages
**Invite Blocking** — Prevents unauthorized server advertising
**Caps & Mention Protection** — Stops disruptive shouting and mass pings
**Link Protection** — Detects suspicious and masked links
**Emoji & Sticker Limits** — Prevents visual spam
**Anti-Raid & Anti-Nuke** — Detects and responds to coordinated attacks
**Alt Account Detection** — Flags suspicious new accounts automatically

> Set it once. Let the system protect your server 24/7.

**Temporary and permanent bans**
