Use it inside OBS

OBS has a built-in Custom Browser Docks feature. Paste your CubDeck URL and it becomes a panel right inside OBS — no second screen needed.

  1. Open OBS → View → Docks → Custom Browser Docks
  2. Add a new dock with your CubDeck URL
  3. Dock it wherever you like in OBS
