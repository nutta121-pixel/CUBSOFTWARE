When you use our Link Shortener service:

  • We track click statistics (number of clicks) on shortened links

6. Authentication (Discord & Twitch OAuth)

CUB SOFTWARE uses a unified login system supporting both Discord and Twitch OAuth. When you log in:

  • We store a session cookie to keep you authenticated across visits (only if Essential cookies are enabled in your cookie preferences)
  • We store your provider (Discord or Twitch), user ID, display name, and avatar URL in your session
  • You may link a second account (e.g. link Twitch to your Discord login); both accounts' identifiers are then associated in your session
  • We do not store your OAuth access tokens beyond the current session, except where a Twitch token is required for live chat functionality (Multi-Twitch)
  • We never access your email, direct messages, or private account information beyond what is described above
  • You can sign out at any time, which clears your session data from our servers

6a. Discord Bots (QuestCord, CleanMe & CUB Protector)

6a.1 QuestCord

When you use QuestCord, we collect and store:

  • Discord user ID and username for player identification
  • Server ID for server-specific features
  • Game statistics: quests completed, currency earned, inventory, levels, achievements

We do NOT access or store your Discord messages, private information, or any data beyond what is necessary for game functionality.

6a.2 CleanMe

When you use CleanMe, we collect and store:

  • Server ID for identifying saved configurations
  • Server structure data: role names, channel names, category names, and permission settings (when you use /save)
  • User ID of the person who saved the configuration

We do NOT access or store your Discord messages, member lists, message history, or any content beyond server structure. Saved configurations may be shared with other users via the CleanMe Website.

6a.3 CUB Protector

When you use CUB Protector, we collect and store:

  • Server ID and all configuration settings you apply through the dashboard or bot commands
  • Moderation action logs (bans, kicks, timeouts, warnings) including user IDs, moderator IDs, reasons, and timestamps, for audit and history purposes
  • XP and leveling data: user IDs, XP points, levels, and role reward assignments per server
  • Ticket data: ticket channel IDs, opener user IDs, and ticket configuration settings
  • Giveaway data: giveaway configuration, entry user IDs, and winner selections
  • Auto-translation settings: configured channel IDs and language preferences. Message content is transmitted to third-party translation APIs (Google Translate, LibreTranslate, MyMemory) for translation but is not stored by us
  • Reaction board settings: channel IDs and emoji configuration
  • Dynamic voice channel configuration and active channel records
  • Logging configuration: which events are logged and to which channels
  • Custom Bot configuration: your Discord bot token (stored encrypted at rest), bot name, and process status. Your token is never exposed publicly and is used solely to run your custom bot instance

We do NOT access or store the content of Discord messages beyond what is necessary for active moderation rule matching. Message content used for moderation checks (e.g. spam detection, link filtering) is evaluated in memory and not persisted.

6a.4 CubReactive

CubReactive is a voice-reactive streaming overlay tool. When you use CubReactive, we collect and store:

  • Discord user ID, username, and avatar URL, obtained via Discord OAuth
  • Voice state data transmitted by the CUB Protector bot: speaking status, muted status, and deafened status. This data is processed in real time to drive overlay animations and is not permanently stored
  • Overlay configuration settings: avatar images you upload, visual settings (border, glow, animations, position, etc.), and overlay preferences
  • A randomly generated overlay key used to authenticate your browser source URL. This key can be regenerated at any time from your dashboard
  • Images you upload for your overlay are stored on our servers and associated with your Discord user ID

Access to CubReactive requires membership in the official CUB SOFTWARE Discord server. We verify this at the time of access using the Discord API. We do not store the result of this check beyond your current session.

6b. Affiliate Program

When you submit an affiliate application or participate in the CUB SOFTWARE Affiliate Program, we collect and store:

  • Application data: Your name or business name, contact email, social media / streaming platform links, content type, estimated monthly viewers or followers, and your described promotion plan
  • Account identifiers: Your Discord user ID (used to link your application to your account)
  • Your assigned vanity code and affiliate link
  • Click statistics: Total clicks, unique visitor fingerprints (anonymised), referrer URLs, and timestamps
  • Commission and payout records

Applications that are not approved are retained for a reasonable period for fraud prevention before being deleted. Visitor fingerprints used for unique-click tracking are anonymised hashes and are not linked to individual identities. This data is used solely to calculate commissions and prevent click fraud.

8. CubVault Password Manager

CubVault is designed with privacy as a core principle:

  • All passwords and sensitive data are encrypted locally on your device using your master password
  • We CANNOT access your master password or decrypt your vault contents
  • If you enable optional cloud sync, your data is encrypted BEFORE leaving your device (end-to-end encryption)
  • We have no ability to recover your data if you forget your master password
  • Your encrypted vault data is stored locally in your browser's localStorage

10. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

We use cookies and similar browser storage technologies to maintain your session, remember your preferences, and understand how our Services are used. When you first visit CUB SOFTWARE, a cookie consent banner is shown that lets you choose exactly which categories of cookies you allow.

We use three categories of cookies:

  • Essential Cookies (optional): Required for core functionality including session management, login authentication, and security. Two cookies are used: session (current session state) and cub_remember (persistent login token, set on each login, valid for up to 30 days, deleted on logout). If disabled, you can still use the site and log in, but you will not remain signed in between visits — your session clears when you close the browser.
  • Functional Cookies (optional): Remember your preferences and settings across sessions to improve your experience. Only set if you have accepted this category.
  • Analytics & Tracking Cookies (optional): Help us understand how users interact with our Services. Also powers affiliate referral click tracking via the __cub_vid visitor identifier cookie. Only set if you have accepted this category.

Your consent choice is stored in your browser's localStorage under the key cub_cookie_prefs. All three categories are optional — if you disable Essential cookies, your session will not persist between visits (you will need to sign in each time). You can change your preferences at any time using the cookie settings button that appears on every page after making an initial choice.
